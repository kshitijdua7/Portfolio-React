Echosight

Hundreds of millions of people live with serious vision loss. Echosight turns what a camera sees into sound: it sweeps left to right, high objects sound high, low objects sound low, and louder means a stronger edge. Your brain does the rest.

Headphones recommended. Video stays on your device and is never uploaded. This is an experiment, not a mobility aid, so it should not replace a cane, a guide dog or training.
