Lifeline

When someone's heart stops, the people nearby often freeze or push at the wrong speed. Lifeline turns any phone into a calm rhythm coach, so you always know whether to go faster, slower, or hold steady.

Concept for practice only, not a medical device. In a real emergency, call your local emergency number first and follow the dispatcher's instructions. Target of 100–120 compressions per minute follows published resuscitation guidelines.
